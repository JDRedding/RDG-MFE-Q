RDG Substrate — 7 DOFs
----------------------

1. Unity (Micro)
| The indivisible relational point; no location, no coordinates.

2. Duality / Opposition
| Tension, contrast, reversible relation; the first structural distinction.

3. Relational Extension
| The ability to form structured triads; minimal geometry without space.

4. Dynamical Variation
| Change, flow, evolution; the substrate’s time-like DOF.

5. Meso-Complexity
| Stable multi-relation patterns; the domain where classical models live.

6. Constraint / Closure
| Conservation, invariance, stability; the substrate’s “lawfulness.”

7. Completeness (Macro)
| Global relational consistency; the whole-system DOF.

The relational substrate of Closure‑Generated Geometry begins with a single locationless point \(P_0\).  
Mirroring \(P_0\) produces its dual, forming the first couple (duality).  
Mirroring this couple generates a second couple whose relations close with the first, yielding the minimal non‑degenerate triad... the first stable relational closure. This establishes the effective micro‑dimension 3, the smallest structure capable of supporting curvature, non‑collinearity, and non‑trivial relational dynamics.

To support persistent meso‑level patterns... attractors, flows, classical‑like stability... the triadic unit must extend into a manifold of at least dimension 5.  
Dimension 5 is the smallest space in which micro‑closures can propagate without collapsing, where local relational variations can coexist, and where meso‑scale pattern stability becomes possible.

Global macro‑completeness... the simultaneous, independent coexistence of micro‑closures (3), meso‑pattern stability (5), and scale‑free global consistency... is first achievable at dimension 7.  
Seven is the smallest relational manifold in which these three regimes can be embedded without contradiction, degeneracy, or forced redundancy. Below 7, at least one regime collapses or becomes dependent on another. Above 7, additional dimensions introduce only derivative structure: higher symmetries, richer projections, and expanded combinatorics, but no new substrate‑level modes of being.

Thus, 7 is the minimal generating dimension of the relational substrate.  
Every domain... physical, cognitive, informational, symbolic... is a projection generated by domain‑specific closure acts on this 7‑dimensional relational manifold, whose seven independent invariants constitute the substrate’s degrees of freedom.
