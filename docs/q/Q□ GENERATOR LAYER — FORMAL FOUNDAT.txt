=====================================================================

Define an equivalence relation ~ on 𝒮:

    s₁ ~ s₂   ⇔   for all meso-layer projections,

Definition:
    G := 𝒮 / ~

Interpretation:

    • Elements g ∈ G are generator configurations.
    • G inherits irrationality + irrelationality from Q□.

=====================================================================

    R : G → I

        ∃ g₁ ≠ g₂ with R(g₁) = R(g₂).

(3) Layer compatibility:
        R(g) ∈ I is rational + partitionable.

Interpretation:
R is the only visibility mechanism from generator to meso-layer.
All SID/PED-visible structure exists only on the image side I.

6. DEFINITION OF Q (QUOTIENT / BOX OPERATOR)
=====================================================================

Let I be the meso-layer (rational, partitionable, non-relational).
Let Q be the rational boundary layer.

Definition:
    Q : I → Q

Properties:

(1) Rationalization:
        Q(i) is symbolically specifiable.

(2) Quotienting:
        i₁ ≈ i₂   ⇔   Q(i₁) = Q(i₂).

(3) Non-invertibility:
        Q is many-to-one; no operator on Q reconstructs i or g.

(4) Layer compatibility:
        Q(i) has no relational structure.

Interpretation:
Q is the rational quotient of I.
Q is the first operator that enforces rationality and removes
generator-side degeneracy.
