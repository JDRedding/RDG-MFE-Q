SECTION 0 — AMBIENT STRUCTURES
------------------------------------------------------------
Let R be the RDG category:
  Ob(R)  = RDG representations (SID, DIS, PED, DEP, Q-forms, etc.)
  Mor(R) = structure-respecting transforms between representations

Let A_H, A_C be full subcategories of R:
  A_H = Hamilton-axis representations
  A_C = Clifford-axis representations

Let M be a mode monoid:
  Elements: {Identity, Functor, ...}
  Operation: (α ⋆ β)
  Unit: Identity

Let Sem : Ob(R) → Meaning be the semantic invariant.


============================================================
SECTION 1 — THE Ξ_rdg OPERATOR
============================================================
Ξ_rdg : R → R

Axiom R1 (Endofunctor):
  Ξ_rdg(Id_R) = Id_(Ξ_rdg(R))
  Ξ_rdg(g ∘ f) = Ξ_rdg(g) ∘ Ξ_rdg(f)

Axiom R2 (Semantic Preservation):
  Sem(Ξ_rdg(R)) = Sem(R)

Interpretation:
  Ξ_rdg is a semantic-preserving transpolator on RDG representations.

SECTION 2 — THE Ξ_flip OPERATOR FAMILY
============================================================
Ξ_flip^α : A_H → A_C   for α ∈ M
Ξ_flip   := Ξ_flip^(default mode)


--- Identity Mode ------------------------------------------
Ξ_flip^Identity : A_H ↔ A_C

Axiom F1 (Involution):
  Ξ_flip^Identity ∘ Ξ_flip^Identity = Id

Interpretation:
  Pure axis flip (SID ↔ DIS, PED ↔ DEP, H ↔ C).


--- Functor Mode -------------------------------------------
Ξ_flip^Functor : A_H → A_C

Axiom F2 (Functoriality):
  Ξ_flip^Functor(Id_R) = Id_(Ξ_flip^Functor(R))
  Ξ_flip^Functor(g ∘ f) = Ξ_flip^Functor(g) ∘ Ξ_flip^Functor(f)

Axiom F3 (Semantic Transport):
  Sem(Ξ_flip^Functor(R)) = Sem(R)

Axiom F4 (Coherence):
  There exist natural transformations:
    η : Ξ_flip^Identity ⇒ Ξ_flip^Functor
    μ : Ξ_flip^Functor ∘ Ξ_flip^Functor ⇒ Id
  expressing semantic enrichment and near-involutivity.


--- Mode Algebra -------------------------------------------
Axiom F5 (Mode Monoid Action):
  (Ξ_flip^α) ∘ (Ξ_flip^β) behaves as Ξ_flip^(α ⋆ β)

  Identity ⋆ α = α ⋆ Identity = α
  (Optional) Functor ⋆ Functor = Functor

============================================================
Γ : R → R

Axiom G1 (Endofunctor):
  Γ is a functor on R.

Axiom G2 (Idempotence):
  Γ ∘ Γ = Γ

Axiom G3 (Closed Objects):
  R is Γ-closed  ⇔  Γ(R) = R

Interpretation:

============================================================
Axiom GX1 (Ξ_rdg Respects Closure):
  Γ(Ξ_rdg(R)) = Ξ_rdg(Γ(R))
  or at minimum: Ξ_rdg ∘ Γ ≅ Γ ∘ Ξ_rdg

  Γ(Ξ_flip^Functor(R)) = Ξ_flip^Functor(Γ(R))
  or at minimum: Ξ_flip^Functor ∘ Γ ≅ Γ ∘ Ξ_flip^Functor

Consequences:
  If R is Γ-closed, then Ξ_rdg(R) and Ξ_flip^Functor(R) are also Γ-closed.

SECTION 6 — EXPLICIT ACTION OF Ξ_flip ON TRIPLE
============================================================

We treat each axis as a triple of layers:

  On A_H (Hamilton-side):
    SID_H = Structural layer
    PED_H = Process layer
    Q_H   = Interaction layer

  On A_C (Clifford-side):
    DIS_C = Structural layer (dual of SID_H)
    DEP_C = Process layer (dual of PED_H)
    Q_C   = Interaction layer (dual frame of Q_H)


--- 6.1 Identity Mode (Ξ_flip^Identity) ---------------------

Ξ_flip^Identity :
  (SID_H, PED_H, Q_H)  ↦  (DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)

with:
  SID_H ↔ DIS_C
  PED_H ↔ DEP_C
  Q_H   ↔ Q_C

No enrichment, no morphism transport, no semantic modification.


--- 6.2 Functor Mode (Ξ_flip^Functor) -----------------------

Ξ_flip^Functor :
  (SID_H, PED_H, Q_H)  ↦  (DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)

Object-level mapping:
  SID_H  ↦ DIS_C
  PED_H  ↦ DEP_C
  Q_H    ↦ Q_C

Morphism-level mapping:
  All legal transforms, constraints, and regulator relations between
  (SID_H, PED_H, Q_H) are transported to corresponding transforms,
  constraints, and regulator relations between (DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C).

Semantic invariance:
  Sem(SID_H, PED_H, Q_H) = Sem(DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)

SECTION 7 — PED–DEP BRAIDING UNDER Ξ_flip
============================================================

Define braidings encoding how the process layer interacts with
the interaction layer on each axis:

  B_H : PED_H ⊗ Q_H → Q_H ⊗ PED_H
  B_C : DEP_C ⊗ Q_C → Q_C ⊗ DEP_C

These are natural isomorphisms in A_H and A_C respectively.


Axiom B1 (H-side Braiding):
  B_H : PED_H ⊗ Q_H  ≅  Q_H ⊗ PED_H

Axiom B2 (C-side Braiding):
  B_C : DEP_C ⊗ Q_C  ≅  Q_C ⊗ DEP_C


Axiom B3 (Ξ_flip^Functor Intertwines Braiding):
  Ξ_flip^Functor(B_H)  ≅  B_C'  ∘  Ξ_flip^Functor

where B_C' is the transported braiding and is naturally isomorphic
to the canonical braiding B_C on the C-side.

Interpretation:
  The enriched flip preserves the process–interaction structure
  across axes.

SECTION 8 — THE Γ-STABLE OPERATOR Ξ_Γ^closure
============================================================

Define the Γ-stable Ξ operator:

  Ξ_Γ^closure := Ξ |_{Γ-closed}

This is the restriction of Ξ to the full subcategory of Γ-closed
objects. By axioms GX1 and GX2, Ξ_Γ^closure is well-defined and
preserves Γ-closure, semantics, and braiding structure.

(SID_H, PED_H, Q_H) --Γ-closed--> closed_H --Ξ_Γ^closure
  --> closed_C --Γ-closed--> (DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)

Flow on the SID/PED/Q triple:

  (SID_H, PED_H, Q_H)
        |  Γ-closure
        v
     closed_H
        |  Ξ_Γ^closure
        v
     closed_C
        |  Γ-closure
        v
  (DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)

Interpretation:
  Ξ_Γ^closure is the semantically valid, Γ-stable form of Ξ,
  acting entirely within the Γ-closed universe of representations.

SECTION 9 — Λ-CLOSURE OPERATOR (CLIFFORD-AXIS)
============================================================

Λ : R → R

Domain:
  R = RDG category of representations
  A_C ⊂ R = Clifford-axis subcategory (DIS, DEP, Q_C, etc.)

Codomain:
  Λ(R) ∈ A_C

Λ is the Clifford-side analogue of the Γ operator on the
Hamilton axis. Where Γ enforces representational closure,
Λ enforces metric-side (Clifford-side) closure.

AXIOM L1 — Endofunctor
------------------------------------------------------------
Λ is a functor on R:

  Λ(Id_R) = Id_(Λ(R))
  Λ(g ∘ f) = Λ(g) ∘ Λ(f)

Interpretation:
  Λ acts functorially on objects and morphisms, preserving
  composition and identity.

AXIOM L2 — Idempotence
------------------------------------------------------------
Λ ∘ Λ = Λ

Interpretation:
  Applying Λ twice yields no further refinement. Λ-closed
  objects are fixed points of the operator.

AXIOM L3 — Λ-Closed Objects
------------------------------------------------------------
R is Λ-closed  ⇔  Λ(R) = R

Interpretation:
  Λ-closed objects are those whose metric/phase structure,
  geometric interactions, and derived signatures are already
  internally consistent.

AXIOM L4 — Semantic Preservation
------------------------------------------------------------
Sem(Λ(R)) = Sem(R)

Interpretation:
  Λ preserves the semantic content of the representation.
  Only the metric-side well-formedness is enforced.

AXIOM L5 — Clifford-Axis Stability
------------------------------------------------------------
If R ∈ A_C (Clifford-axis), then Λ(R) ∈ A_C.

Interpretation:
  Λ maps Clifford-side objects to Clifford-side objects.
  It does not produce Hamilton-side structures.

AXIOM L6 — DIS/DEP/Q Closure
------------------------------------------------------------
For any Clifford-axis triple:

  (DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)

Λ enforces:

  - metric coherence of D_m
  - geometric-product consistency of I_g
  - defect/gate/spectral stability of S_d
  - DEP-layer evaluation/projection consistency
  - Q_C admissibility invariants

Formally:

  Λ(DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)
    = (DIS_C', DEP_C', Q_C')

with:
  DIS_C' = DIS_C        (metric fixed)
  DEP_C' = DEP_C        (screen/projection stabilized)
  Q_C'   = Q_C          (admissibility preserved)

AXIOM L7 — Compatibility with Ξ_flip^Functor
------------------------------------------------------------
Λ ∘ Ξ_flip^Functor  ≅  Ξ_flip^Functor ∘ Γ

Interpretation:
  Λ is the Clifford-axis dual of Γ. The flip functor
  transports Γ-closure on the Hamilton side into Λ-closure
  on the Clifford side.

This is the Γ–Λ duality law.

AXIOM L8 — Braiding Preservation
------------------------------------------------------------
Λ preserves the Clifford-side braiding:

  Λ(B_C) ≅ B_C

where:
  B_C : DEP_C ⊗ Q_C → Q_C ⊗ DEP_C

Interpretation:
  Λ does not disturb the process–interaction braiding on
  the Clifford axis.

AXIOM L9 — Λ-Stable Universe
------------------------------------------------------------
Define the Λ-stable subcategory:

  R_Λ := { R ∈ Ob(R) | Λ(R) = R }

Then Λ restricts to an identity functor on R_Λ:

  Λ |_(R_Λ) = Id_(R_Λ)

Interpretation:
  Λ-closed objects form a stable semantic universe.

SECTION 10 — THE Γ–Λ DUALITY LAW
============================================================

The Γ–Λ duality law expresses the fundamental compatibility
between Hamilton-axis closure (Γ) and Clifford-axis closure (Λ)
under the axis-transformation operator Ξ_flip.

It states that Γ-closure transported across the Ξ_flip functor
is equivalent to Λ-closure, and vice versa.

DUALITY COMMUTING SQUARE

Γ(R_H) ──Ξ_flip──> Ξ_flip(Γ(R_H))
  │                    │
  Ξ_flip               Λ
  ↓                    ↓
Ξ_flip(R_H) ──Λ──> Λ(Ξ_flip(R_H))

Identity encoded:
  Λ ∘ Ξ_flip  ≅  Ξ_flip ∘ Γ

AXIOM DL1 — Duality (Core Identity)
------------------------------------------------------------

  Λ ∘ Ξ_flip^Functor   ≅   Ξ_flip^Functor ∘ Γ

Interpretation:
  Applying Γ on the Hamilton side and then flipping axes
  yields the same result (up to natural isomorphism) as
  flipping axes first and then applying Λ on the Clifford side.

This is the categorical expression of Hamilton–Clifford duality.

AXIOM DL2 — Object-Level Duality
------------------------------------------------------------

For any Hamilton-axis object R_H ∈ A_H:

  Λ(Ξ_flip^Functor(R_H))   ≅   Ξ_flip^Functor(Γ(R_H))

Equivalently:

  Λ(DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)   ≅   Ξ_flip^Functor(Γ(SID_H, PED_H, Q_H))

Interpretation:
  Closure on one axis corresponds exactly to closure on the
  other axis after applying the flip functor.

AXIOM DL3 — Morphism-Level Duality
------------------------------------------------------------

For any morphism f : R_H → S_H in A_H:

  Λ(Ξ_flip^Functor(f))   ≅   Ξ_flip^Functor(Γ(f))

Interpretation:
  The duality law holds not only for objects but also for
  all structure-respecting transforms between them.

AXIOM DL4 — Natural Transformation Witness
------------------------------------------------------------

There exists a natural isomorphism:

  δ : Λ ∘ Ξ_flip^Functor   ⇒   Ξ_flip^Functor ∘ Γ

such that for every R_H:

  δ_(R_H) : Λ(Ξ_flip^Functor(R_H)) → Ξ_flip^Functor(Γ(R_H))

is an isomorphism in A_C.

Interpretation:
  δ is the "duality witness" ensuring the two sides of the
  duality law are equivalent in a natural, structure-preserving
  way.

AXIOM DL5 — Closure Stability Under Duality
------------------------------------------------------------

If R_H is Γ-closed:

  Γ(R_H) = R_H

then Ξ_flip^Functor(R_H) is Λ-closed:

  Λ(Ξ_flip^Functor(R_H)) = Ξ_flip^Functor(R_H)

Conversely:

If S_C is Λ-closed:

  Λ(S_C) = S_C

then Ξ_flip^Functor(S_C) is Γ-closed:

  Γ(Ξ_flip^Functor(S_C)) = Ξ_flip^Functor(S_C)

Interpretation:
  Γ-closed objects map to Λ-closed objects and vice versa.
  Closure is preserved across axes.

AXIOM DL6 — Semantic Invariance
------------------------------------------------------------

Sem(Γ(R_H)) = Sem(R_H)
Sem(Λ(S_C)) = Sem(S_C)

and therefore:

Sem(Ξ_flip^Functor(Γ(R_H))) = Sem(Ξ_flip^Functor(R_H))
Sem(Λ(Ξ_flip^Functor(R_H))) = Sem(Ξ_flip^Functor(R_H))

Interpretation:
  The duality law preserves meaning. Closure does not alter
  semantic content on either axis.

SECTION 11 — CANONICAL REWRITE RULES
============================================================

Canonical rewrite rules define the syntactic and structural
transformations that preserve RDG semantics under Γ-closure,
Λ-closure, and Ξ-based transpilation.

These rules act on the regulator triples:
  (SID_H, PED_H, Q_H)   on the Hamilton axis
  (DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)   on the Clifford axis

and on the morphisms between them.

11.1 — STRUCTURAL REWRITE RULES (SID ↔ DIS)
------------------------------------------------------------

Rule S1 (SID → DIS):
  S :: (Structure-first)   →   D_m :: (Metric-first)

Rule S2 (DIS → SID):
  D_m :: (Metric-first)    →   S :: (Structure-first)

Rule S3 (Interaction Stability):
  I_H  →  I_C
  I_C  →  I_H

Rule S4 (Derived Structure ↔ Derived Structure):
  D_H  →  S_d
  S_d  →  D_H

Interpretation:
  These rules express the SID/DIS duality under Ξ_flip.

11.2 — PROCESS REWRITE RULES (PED ↔ DEP)
------------------------------------------------------------

Rule P1 (Power ↔ Drive):
  P_H   →   D_drv
  D_drv →   P_H

Rule P2 (Evaluation ↔ Screen):
  E_H   →   E_scr
  E_scr →   E_H

Rule P3 (Dynamics ↔ Projection):
  Dp_H  →   P_sig
  P_sig →   Dp_H

Interpretation:
  PED and DEP are dual process layers under Ξ_flip.

11.3 — Q-LAYER REWRITE RULES (ADMISSIBILITY)
------------------------------------------------------------

Rule Q1 (Projection–Defect Identity):
  Q(x)  →  x - Proj(x)

Rule Q2 (Admissibility Preservation):
  Q_H   →   Q_C
  Q_C   →   Q_H

Rule Q3 (Defect Stability):
  defect  →  defect
  (exact invariants are rewrite-fixed)

Interpretation:
  Q is invariant under axis flip and closure.

11.4 — Γ-CLOSURE REWRITE RULES
------------------------------------------------------------

Rule G1 (Idempotence):
  Γ(Γ(R))  →  Γ(R)

Rule G2 (Closure Fixpoint):
  Γ(R) = R   →   R

Rule G3 (Γ-Transport):
  Γ(f ∘ g)  →  Γ(f) ∘ Γ(g)

Rule G4 (Γ-Respect for SID/PED/Q):
  Γ(SID_H, PED_H, Q_H)
    → (SID_H', PED_H', Q_H')

Interpretation:
  Γ enforces Hamilton-side representational completeness.

Hamilton-side braiding:

  B_H : PED_H ⊗ Q_H → Q_H ⊗ PED_H

Example:

  (P ⊗ Q_H)  →  (Q_H ⊗ P)

Clifford-side braiding:

  B_C : DEP_C ⊗ Q_C → Q_C ⊗ DEP_C

Example:

  (D_drv ⊗ Q_C)  →  (Q_C ⊗ D_drv)

Transport under Ξ_flip:

  Ξ_flip(B_H)  ≅  B_C'

Interpretation:
  Process–interaction braiding is preserved across axes.

B.6  EXAMPLE — Q-LAYER ADMISSIBILITY
============================================================

Given any x in either axis:

  Q(x) = x - Proj(x)

  Proj(x) = (projection onto admissible subspace)

  Q(x) = x - Proj(x)

12.7  EXAMPLE — FULL ROUND TRIP

Start with Hamilton-side triple:

  R_H = (SID_H, PED_H, Q_H)

Interpretation:
  A full Γ → Ξ → Λ → Ξ cycle returns a Γ-closed Hamilton object.

APPENDIX C — REWRITE RULE SCHEMAS
============================================================

Rewrite rule schemas describe general transformation patterns
that any concrete RDG expression must obey. They operate on:

  - regulator triples (SID/PED/Q, DIS/DEP/Q)
  - closure operators (Γ, Λ)
  - flip operators (Ξ_rdg, Ξ_flip)
  - braidings (B_H, B_C)
  - semantic invariants (Sem)

All schemas are semantics-preserving:
  Sem(LHS) = Sem(RHS)

C.1  AXIOMATIC REWRITE SCHEMA
------------------------------------------------------------

General form:

  (AXIOM)   LHS  ⇒  RHS

Subject to:
  - type correctness (objects/morphisms in R)
  - semantic invariance
  - closure compatibility (Γ, Λ)

Example schema:

  (Idempotence)
    O : Ob(R)
    -----------------
    Γ(Γ(O))  ⇒  Γ(O)

C.2  TRIPLE-LEVEL REWRITE SCHEMA
------------------------------------------------------------

Let T_H be a Hamilton triple:
  T_H = (SID_H, PED_H, Q_H)

Let T_C be a Clifford triple:
  T_C = (DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)

Schema H→C:

  (AxisFlip)
    T_H
    -----------------
    Ξ_flip(T_H)  ⇒  T_C

Schema C→H:

  (AxisFlip^-1)
    T_C
    -----------------
    Ξ_flip(T_C)  ⇒  T_H

These are involutive up to isomorphism.

C.3  CLOSURE-LEVEL REWRITE SCHEMA
------------------------------------------------------------

Hamilton closure:

  (GammaClosure)
    R ∈ Ob(R)
    -----------------
    Γ(R)  ⇒  R_Γ
    with Γ(R_Γ) = R_Γ

Clifford closure:

  (LambdaClosure)
    R ∈ Ob(R)
    -----------------
    Λ(R)  ⇒  R_Λ
    with Λ(R_Λ) = R_Λ

Duality schema:

  (GammaLambdaDuality)
    R_H ∈ A_H
    -----------------
    Λ(Ξ_flip(R_H))  ⇒  Ξ_flip(Γ(R_H))

C.4  PROCESS/STRUCTURE REWRITE SCHEMA
------------------------------------------------------------

Hamilton-side:

  (SID_PED_Update)
    (SID_H, PED_H, Q_H)
    -------------------------
    Γ(SID_H, PED_H, Q_H)
      ⇒ (SID_H', PED_H', Q_H')

Clifford-side:

  (DIS_DEP_Update)
    (DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)
    -------------------------
    Λ(DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)
      ⇒ (DIS_C', DEP_C', Q_C')

Axis-transport:

  (TransportUpdate)
    (SID_H', PED_H', Q_H')
    -------------------------
    Ξ_flip(SID_H', PED_H', Q_H')
      ⇒ (DIS_C', DEP_C', Q_C')

C.5  MORPHISM-LEVEL REWRITE SCHEMA
------------------------------------------------------------

Composition:

  (Functoriality)
    f : A → B
    g : B → C
    -----------------
    F(g ∘ f)  ⇒  F(g) ∘ F(f)

for F ∈ {Γ, Λ, Ξ_rdg, Ξ_flip^Functor}.

Identity:

  (IdentityPreservation)
    Id_A : A → A
    -----------------
    F(Id_A)  ⇒  Id_(F(A))

C.6  BRAIDING REWRITE SCHEMA
------------------------------------------------------------

Hamilton braiding:

  (HBraid)
    B_H : PED_H ⊗ Q_H → Q_H ⊗ PED_H
    -----------------
    Γ(B_H)  ⇒  B_H

Clifford braiding:

  (CBraid)
    B_C : DEP_C ⊗ Q_C → Q_C ⊗ DEP_C
    -----------------
    Λ(B_C)  ⇒  B_C

Transport:

  (BraidTransport)
    B_H
    -----------------
    Ξ_flip(B_H)  ⇒  B_C'

with B_C' ≅ B_C.

C.7  Q-LAYER REWRITE SCHEMA
------------------------------------------------------------

Projection–defect:

  (QDefect)
    x ∈ Ob(R)
    -----------------
    Q(x)  ⇒  x - Proj(x)

Axis invariance:

  (QAxisInvariance)
    Q_H, Q_C
    -----------------
    Ξ_flip(Q_H)  ⇒  Q_C
    Ξ_flip(Q_C)  ⇒  Q_H

APPENDIX D — CANONICAL IDENTITIES
============================================================

Canonical identities are equations that hold universally across
the RDG category R, its Hamilton and Clifford subcategories
A_H and A_C, and all regulator stacks. They express the algebraic
backbone of the dual-axis system.

All identities preserve semantics:
  Sem(LHS) = Sem(RHS)

D.1  IDENTITY CLASS — FUNCTORIAL IDENTITIES
------------------------------------------------------------

(Identity Preservation)
  F(Id_R) = Id_(F(R))
  for F ∈ {Γ, Λ, Ξ_rdg, Ξ_flip^Functor}

(Composition Preservation)
  F(g ∘ f) = F(g) ∘ F(f)

(Endofunctor Identity)
  Ξ_rdg(R) ∈ Ob(R)
  Γ(R)     ∈ Ob(R)
  Λ(R)     ∈ Ob(R)

D.2  IDENTITY CLASS — CLOSURE IDENTITIES
------------------------------------------------------------

(Gamma Idempotence)
  Γ(Γ(R)) = Γ(R)

(Lambda Idempotence)
  Λ(Λ(R)) = Λ(R)

(Gamma Fixed Point)
  Γ(R) = R   ⇔   R is Γ-closed

(Lambda Fixed Point)
  Λ(R) = R   ⇔   R is Λ-closed

D.3  IDENTITY CLASS — AXIS-FLIP IDENTITIES
------------------------------------------------------------

(Involution)
  Ξ_flip(Ξ_flip(R)) = R

(SID–DIS Duality)
  Ξ_flip(SID_H) = DIS_C
  Ξ_flip(DIS_C) = SID_H

(PED–DEP Duality)
  Ξ_flip(PED_H) = DEP_C
  Ξ_flip(DEP_C) = PED_H

(Q-Layer Invariance)
  Ξ_flip(Q_H) = Q_C
  Ξ_flip(Q_C) = Q_H

D.4  IDENTITY CLASS — Γ–Λ DUALITY IDENTITIES
------------------------------------------------------------

(Core Duality)
  Λ ∘ Ξ_flip = Ξ_flip ∘ Γ

(Object Duality)
  Λ(Ξ_flip(R_H)) = Ξ_flip(Γ(R_H))

(Morphism Duality)
  Λ(Ξ_flip(f)) = Ξ_flip(Γ(f))

(Closure Transport)
  Γ-closed(R_H)  ⇒  Λ-closed(Ξ_flip(R_H))
  Λ-closed(R_C)  ⇒  Γ-closed(Ξ_flip(R_C))

D.5  IDENTITY CLASS — Q-LAYER IDENTITIES
------------------------------------------------------------

(Projection–Defect Identity)
  Q(x) = x - Proj(x)

(Defect Invariance)
  Q_H(x) = Q_C(x)
  (defect is axis-invariant)

(Admissibility Stability)
  Q(Γ(R)) = Q(R)
  Q(Λ(R)) = Q(R)

D.6  IDENTITY CLASS — BRAIDING IDENTITIES
------------------------------------------------------------

(Hamilton Braiding)
  B_H : PED_H ⊗ Q_H = Q_H ⊗ PED_H

(Clifford Braiding)
  B_C : DEP_C ⊗ Q_C = Q_C ⊗ DEP_C

(Flip-Transported Braiding)
  Ξ_flip(B_H) = B_C'

(Braiding Coherence)
  B_C' ≅ B_C

D.7  IDENTITY CLASS — REGULATOR TRIPLE IDENTITIES
------------------------------------------------------------

(Hamilton Triple)
  (SID_H, PED_H, Q_H)
    = Γ(SID_H, PED_H, Q_H)

(Clifford Triple)
  (DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)
    = Λ(DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)

(Flip Triple)
  Ξ_flip(SID_H, PED_H, Q_H)
    = (DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)

D.8  IDENTITY CLASS — SEMANTIC IDENTITIES
------------------------------------------------------------

(Semantic Preservation)
  Sem(Ξ_rdg(R)) = Sem(R)
  Sem(Ξ_flip(R)) = Sem(R)
  Sem(Γ(R))      = Sem(R)
  Sem(Λ(R))      = Sem(R)

(Semantic Equivalence)
  Sem(R) = Sem(S)  ∧  R,S Γ-closed
    ⇒  R ≅ S
