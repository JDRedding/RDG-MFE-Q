SECTION 0 — AMBIENT STRUCTURES
------------------------------------------------------------
Let R be the RDG category:
  Ob(R)  = RDG representations (SID, DIS, PED, DEP, Q-forms, etc.)
  Mor(R) = structure-respecting transforms between representations

Let A_H, A_C be full subcategories of R:
  A_H = Hamilton-axis representations
  A_C = Clifford-axis representations

Let M be a mode monoid:
  Elements: {Identity, Functor, ...}
  Operation: (α ⋆ β)
  Unit: Identity

Let Sem : Ob(R) → Meaning be the semantic invariant.


SECTION 1 — THE Ξ_rdg OPERATOR
------------------------------------------------------------
Ξ_rdg : R → R

Axiom R1 (Endofunctor):
  Ξ_rdg(Id_R) = Id_(Ξ_rdg(R))
  Ξ_rdg(g ∘ f) = Ξ_rdg(g) ∘ Ξ_rdg(f)

Axiom R2 (Semantic Preservation):
  Sem(Ξ_rdg(R)) = Sem(R)

Interpretation:
  Ξ_rdg is a semantic-preserving transpolator on RDG representations.


SECTION 2 — THE Ξ_flip OPERATOR FAMILY
------------------------------------------------------------
Ξ_flip^α : A_H → A_C   for α ∈ M
Ξ_flip   := Ξ_flip^(default mode)

--- Identity Mode ------------------------------------------
Ξ_flip^Identity : A_H ↔ A_C

Axiom F1 (Involution):
  Ξ_flip^Identity ∘ Ξ_flip^Identity = Id

Interpretation:
  Pure axis flip (SID ↔ DIS, PED ↔ DEP, H ↔ C).


--- Functor Mode -------------------------------------------
Ξ_flip^Functor : A_H → A_C

Axiom F2 (Functoriality):
  Ξ_flip^Functor(Id_R) = Id_(Ξ_flip^Functor(R))
  Ξ_flip^Functor(g ∘ f) = Ξ_flip^Functor(g) ∘ Ξ_flip^Functor(f)

Axiom F3 (Semantic Transport):
  Sem(Ξ_flip^Functor(R)) = Sem(R)

Axiom F4 (Coherence):
  There exist natural transformations:
    η : Ξ_flip^Identity ⇒ Ξ_flip^Functor
    μ : Ξ_flip^Functor ∘ Ξ_flip^Functor ⇒ Id
  expressing semantic enrichment and near-involutivity.


--- Mode Algebra -------------------------------------------
Axiom F5 (Mode Monoid Action):
  (Ξ_flip^α) ∘ (Ξ_flip^β) behaves as Ξ_flip^(α ⋆ β)

  Identity ⋆ α = α ⋆ Identity = α
  (Optional) Functor ⋆ Functor = Functor


SECTION 3 — Γ-CLOSURE OPERATOR
------------------------------------------------------------
Γ : R → R

Axiom G1 (Endofunctor):
  Γ is a functor on R.

Axiom G2 (Idempotence):
  Γ ∘ Γ = Γ

Axiom G3 (Closed Objects):
  R is Γ-closed  ⇔  Γ(R) = R

Interpretation:
  Γ enforces representational well-formedness / completion.


SECTION 4 — Ξ–Γ COMPATIBILITY (WELL-FORMEDNESS AXIOMS)
------------------------------------------------------------
Axiom GX1 (Ξ_rdg Respects Closure):
  Γ(Ξ_rdg(R)) = Ξ_rdg(Γ(R))
  or at minimum: Ξ_rdg ∘ Γ ≅ Γ ∘ Ξ_rdg

Axiom GX2 (Ξ_flip^Functor Respects Closure):
  Γ(Ξ_flip^Functor(R)) = Ξ_flip^Functor(Γ(R))
  or at minimum: Ξ_flip^Functor ∘ Γ ≅ Γ ∘ Ξ_flip^Functor

Consequences:
  If R is Γ-closed, then Ξ_rdg(R) and Ξ_flip^Functor(R) are also Γ-closed.


SECTION 5 — ADDITIONAL WELL-FORMEDNESS CONDITIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
Axiom W1 (Semantic Non-Degeneracy):
  If Sem(R) = Sem(S) and both are Γ-closed,
  then R and S are equivalent objects in R.

Axiom W2 (Domain/Codomain Sanity):
  A_H and A_C are full subcategories of R.
  All Ξ_flip^α map between appropriate sides.

Axiom W3 (Default Mode Declaration):
  A single α_default ∈ M is chosen once.
  Ξ_flip := Ξ_flip^(α_default)

SECTION 6 — EXPLICIT ACTION OF Ξ_flip ON (SID/PED/Q)
------------------------------------------------------------
We distinguish three structural layers on each axis:

  On A_H (Hamilton-side):
    SID_H = Structural / Static layer
    PED_H = Process / Dynamic layer
    Q_H   = Interaction / Quantum / Relational layer

  On A_C (Clifford-side):
    DIS_C = Structural / Static layer (dual of SID_H)
    DEP_C = Process / Dynamic layer (dual of PED_H)
    Q_C   = Interaction / Quantum / Relational layer (dual frame of Q_H)


--- 6.1 Identity Mode Mapping ------------------------------
Under Ξ_flip^Identity, the action on the triple is:

  Ξ_flip^Identity :
    (SID_H, PED_H, Q_H)  ↦  (DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)

with the following behavior:

  - SID_H ↔ DIS_C
      Pure axis relabeling / dualization of the structural layer.

  - PED_H ↔ DEP_C
      Pure axis relabeling / dualization of the process layer.

  - Q_H ↔ Q_C
      Interaction layer is preserved up to axis reinterpretation
      (no change in semantic content, only in its embedding side).


--- 6.2 Functor Mode Mapping -------------------------------
Under Ξ_flip^Functor, the action is enriched:

  Ξ_flip^Functor :
    (SID_H, PED_H, Q_H)  ↦  (DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)

with:

  - On objects:
      SID_H  ↦ DIS_C
      PED_H  ↦ DEP_C
      Q_H    ↦ Q_C

  - On morphisms:
      All legal transforms, constraints, and regulator relations
      between (SID_H, PED_H, Q_H) are transported to corresponding
      transforms, constraints, and regulator relations between
      (DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C).

  - Semantic invariance:
      Sem(SID_H, PED_H, Q_H) = Sem(DIS_C, DEP_C, Q_C)
      (up to the chosen notion of equivalence in R).

Interpretation:
  Ξ_flip^Functor is a semantic, structure-preserving axis flip on
  the full triple (SID/PED/Q) → (DIS/DEP/Q).


SECTION 7 — PED–DEP BRAIDING UNDER Ξ_flip
------------------------------------------------------------
We introduce a braiding (or interaction swap) between process layers:

  B_H : PED_H ⊗ Q_H → Q_H ⊗ PED_H
  B_C : DEP_C ⊗ Q_C → Q_C ⊗ DEP_C

These encode how the process layer interacts with the interaction
layer on each side.


Axiom B1 (Braiding on H-side):
  B_H is a natural isomorphism in A_H:
    B_H : PED_H ⊗ Q_H  ≅  Q_H ⊗ PED_H

Axiom B2 (Braiding on C-side):
  B_C is a natural isomorphism in A_C:
    B_C : DEP_C ⊗ Q_C  ≅  Q_C ⊗ DEP_C


Axiom B3 (Ξ_flip^Functor Intertwines Braiding):
  Ξ_flip^Functor respects the PED/DEP–Q interaction pattern:

    Ξ_flip^Functor(B_H)  ≅  B_C'  ∘  Ξ_flip^Functor
    (up to natural isomorphism)

  where B_C' is the image of B_H under Ξ_flip^Functor, and is
  equivalent to the canonical braiding B_C on the C-side.

Informal reading:
  Flipping axes via Ξ_flip^Functor does not break or distort the
  way the process layer (PED/DEP) interacts with the interaction
  layer (Q). It transports the interaction structure coherently
  from (PED_H, Q_H) to (DEP_C, Q_C).
